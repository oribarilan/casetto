# Changelog

What's new in each version of Casterr, newest first. The app shows these lines in What's New. AGENTS.md says how to add a version.

- The playing episode no longer says Waiting for Wi-Fi.
- Settings says how much space Keep downloaded takes.
- Cassy tells you what's new after an update, and Settings shows the version.
