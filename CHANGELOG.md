# Changelog

What's new in each version of Casterr, newest first. The app shows these lines in What's New. AGENTS.md says how to add a version.

## 0.7.2

- When more episodes are waiting, the Inbox card sits on a little pile, so it looks like a card you can swipe away.

## 0.7.1

- The Inbox card shows its swipe tip once, even if you saw the old tip on the Inbox rows.

## 0.7

- Your newest Inbox episode sits on a big card with its description: swipe it right to queue it or left to remove it.
- The rest of your new episodes wait under it, and the next one moves up onto the card.

## 0.6.1

- The Queue no longer scrolls up and down on its own when you open the app.

## 0.6

- On a show's page, Follow is the one orange button, and Star sits beside it as a round button.
- Choose where a show's new episodes go, and how long they wait in your Inbox, from menus right on its page.
- Those menus appear the moment you tap Follow or Star, and slide in smoothly.

## 0.5.1

- A show's description shows Show more only when there's more to read, and a tap anywhere on it opens it.
- The country in Find Shows and What's New in Settings now look tappable.
- Following, unfollowing and other taps across the app are smoother, even with big shows.

## 0.5

- The star and the + give a little spin when you star or follow a show.
- On a show's page, the Unfollow question now points at the Following button.

## 0.4.1

- Follow, Star, a show's new episodes settings, and Settings respond the moment you tap.

## 0.4

- A show page lists its newest 30 episodes, with Show older episodes for the rest, so shows with a long history open faster.

## 0.3.1

- A show you open from Find Shows shows its name and art at once, and its page keeps its shape while the episodes load.

## 0.3

- Double press your AirPods to bookmark the moment, with a chime, and triple press to skip back 15.
- Settings lets you choose what double and triple press do, and other headphones and car buttons follow the same choices.

## 0.2

- The big pause button in Now Playing fills with the voice as it plays, like the mini player's.
- A shared clip shows its waveform filling as it plays, and stays sharp in WhatsApp.
- Away from Wi-Fi, an episode waiting to download has Download in its actions, which downloads it on cellular.
- The playing episode no longer says Waiting for Wi-Fi.
- Settings says how much space Keep downloaded takes.
- Cassy tells you what's new after an update, and Settings shows the version.

## 0.1

- The first TestFlight build.
