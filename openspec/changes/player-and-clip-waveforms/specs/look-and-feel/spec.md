## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pause meter in Now Playing
While an episode plays, Now Playing's pause button SHALL keep its white circle and fill its two bars with tangerine exactly as the mini player's pause meter does: the same levels, glide, and lag. With Reduce Motion it MUST be the plain pause icon; paused, the play icon.

#### Scenario: Speaking in Now Playing
- **WHEN** the host speaks a loud sentence with Now Playing open
- **THEN** the big pause button's bars fill most of the way, moving with the mini player's

#### Scenario: Reduce Motion in Now Playing
- **WHEN** Reduce Motion is on
- **THEN** Now Playing shows the plain pause icon
