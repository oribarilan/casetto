# Proposal

## Why

The suggestions heading said "Top Shows", which read cold. The interview also asked whether to make suggestions personal. They stay one chart: the list only appears before you follow anything or behind +, Apple's documented chart can only be focused by country, and genre charts exist only on an old, undocumented feed.

## What Changes

- The heading over the top shows says "Popular now".
- Suggestions keep following the phone's region, with no country setting and no personal picks.
